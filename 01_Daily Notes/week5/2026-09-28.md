#### Notes
- Both tools achieve a very good detection at the phylum through family ranks. Most of the differences show up at the genus and species level
- Savont is conservative, with 0 false positives at both species and genus level. It has 6 false negatives (missing taxa). These 6 taxa are low in abundance, and fall below the minimum cluster size set by Savont (12 reads).
- wf-16s produces 9 false positives at the species level (purity 0.526). These are mostly Escherichia/Shigella strain-level taxa that don't exist as entries in the ground truth data. 
This reveals that the greengenes2_plus database is probably significantly different compared to the database Savont uses.
